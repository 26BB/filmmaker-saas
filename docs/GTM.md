# FilmDrop — Go-To-Market Strategy

> **Status**: Draft
> **Company Base**: India
> **Primary Markets**: India (INR) + Global English-speaking (USD)
> **Launch Window**: Week 6 (early November 2026)
> **Market Signal**: Vimeo On Demand shut down Sept 21, 2026 — window is NOW

---

## 1. Strategic Context

This is a **double market opportunity**:

1. **Global**: Vimeo On Demand's shutdown created an immediate, emotionally charged supply of displaced filmmakers actively searching for alternatives
2. **India-native**: No existing platform serves Indian indie filmmakers with INR pricing + UPI payments + regional language discovery

Our GTM runs **three parallel motions**:

| Motion | Target | Goal |
|--------|--------|------|
| **India supply** | Indian indie filmmakers (Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam, Marathi...) | 30 Indian films at launch |
| **Global supply** | Displaced Vimeo On Demand users | 20 international films at launch |
| **Demand seeding** | Film viewers (India + global) | 500 purchases in Month 1 |

## 5. Phase 3 — Growth Loops (Post-Launch)

### 5.1 Filmmaker Virality Loop

```
Filmmaker uploads -> Shares film page (Instagram / WhatsApp) -> Viewer clicks
-> Viewer pays via UPI -> Filmmaker earns INR -> Filmmaker uploads more films
        ^_______________________________________________|
```

**India-specific accelerators**:
- WhatsApp share button on film page (India's #1 sharing channel)
- "Pay via UPI" prominent on film page (lower friction than card)
- Instagram Story share card: film poster + filmmaker name + "Watch on FilmDrop"
- "Powered by FilmDrop" badge (opt-out)

## 10. Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Razorpay Route approval delay | Medium | High | Apply Day 1; Razorpay has fast-track for Indian startups |
| Indian filmmakers skeptical of new platform | Medium | High | Founding Filmmaker guarantee + MAMI community endorsement |
| UPI payment failures (bank downtime) | Low | Medium | Razorpay handles retry; show fallback card option |
| GST non-compliance | Low | High | Register GSTIN before launch; CA review |
| Low viewer conversion in India (price sensitivity) | Medium | High | Rs.99 impulse pricing; UPI removes card friction |
| Competitor fast follower (Indian OTT) | Low | Medium | Speed + community moat; OTTs won't do self-serve |
| Stripe Connect delay (for international) | Low | High | Apply Day 1; run India-only first if needed |
| Content quality issues / pirated content uploads | Medium | Medium | ToS, DMCA + IT Act 2000 takedown process |
