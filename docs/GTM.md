# FilmDrop — Go-To-Market Strategy

> **Scope**: Portfolio project — GTM is aspirational / for when this goes live
> **Launch Window**: After internship + funding
> **Market Signal**: Vimeo On Demand shut down Sept 21, 2026

---

## 1. Strategic Context

Vimeo On Demand's shutdown created an immediate supply of displaced filmmakers actively searching for alternatives. When FilmDrop goes live, there are **two motions**:

| Motion | Target | Goal |
|--------|--------|------|
| **Supply** | Indie filmmakers (global) | Get 50 films uploaded at launch |
| **Demand** | Film viewers | Drive first 500 purchases in Month 1 |

---

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Filmmaker ICP — "The Displaced Creator"

| Signal | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Was on Vimeo On Demand | Active Vimeo Pro/OTT user pre-Sept 2026 |
| Has completed film | Short film, doc, or feature ready to distribute |
| Self-distributes | No deal with MUBI/Fandor |
| Film festival circuit | Sundance, SXSW, Tribeca, BAFTA alumni |
| Revenue expectation | Zero upfront cost + 85% rev share |

### Viewer ICP — "The Niche Film Enthusiast"

| Signal | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Watches Mubi, Criterion | Pays for curated indie content |
| Follows filmmakers on social | Direct creator relationship |
| Price point | $4–$12 per film |

## 3. Pre-Launch — Warm Outreach

### Vimeo On Demand Refugee Targeting

**Channels**:
- Reddit: `r/Filmmakers`, `r/indiefilm`, `r/videography`
- Twitter/X: Search `"Vimeo On Demand" alternative` and reply
- Film school alumni networks

**Message**:
> *"We built FilmDrop because Vimeo On Demand shut down. Upload your film, set your price, keep 85%. No curation, no gatekeeping, zero upfront cost. Beta is open — filmdrop.io"*

**Goal**: 20 filmmakers pre-launch, films ready on Day 1.

### Beta Waitlist

- `/waitlist` page before full launch
- Collect: name, email, film title
- Target: 100 signups before launch
- Email sequence: day 0, day 3, day 7

### Early Adopter Incentive

> **"Founding Filmmaker" Program** — First 100 filmmakers:
> - **90/10 revenue split for life** (vs. standard 85/15)
> - Founding Filmmaker badge on profile

## 5. Growth Loops (Post-Launch)

### Filmmaker Virality Loop

```
Filmmaker uploads → Shares film page on social → Viewer clicks
→ Viewer purchases → Filmmaker earns → Filmmaker uploads more
       ^_______________________________________________|
```

**Accelerators**:
- "Powered by FilmDrop" badge on film pages (opt-out)
- Social share card: film poster + filmmaker name
- Filmmaker referral: "Invite a filmmaker, both get 90/10 for 3 months"
